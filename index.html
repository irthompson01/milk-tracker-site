What it does

  • The fridge: log a milk run when you buy and a container when you finish it. The app always knows what's left and gives you one calm heads-up before the last one's gone.
  • Shared household fridge: invite the people you live with. Everyone sees the same stock, logs milk, and gets the running-low alert. Join a family fridge over the holidays.
  • Milk Run: your usual stores by distance, nearby shops that sell milk, delivery links.
  • Stats, streaks and badges: charts, cow-day equivalents, 23 badges, and a shareable "Milk Wrapped" card every January.
  • Widgets and Siri: one-tap logging from your Home Screen, "Finished a gallon in Milkmaxxer".

FAQ

Where is my data stored? On your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The app has no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I share my fridge? Home → tap the fridge card → "Share this fridge…". Send the link in Messages. The other person needs the app and iCloud.

I get alerts on two devices. Only one device sends them: Settings → Alerts → "Send alerts from this device".

How do I delete my data? Settings → "Delete all entries", or delete the app. Shared fridge data you created is removed when you leave or stop sharing.
